Corrections to upstream guidance:

- Google does not use llms.txt for search visibility; it is not a required score or
  publication gate. Other consumers need their own evidence.
- Google FAQ rich results are no longer shown; FAQPage is not a universal pass/fail
  check. Related questions need not each become a separate page.
- Raw HTML inspection is useful, while JavaScript-rendered indexing requires a
  separate assessment. Missing initial HTML is not a universal invisibility verdict.
- Bot access is chosen by purpose and product policy, without a blanket training
  allow rule. Optional techniques and fixed counts are not universal requirements.
- No automatic publication, account integration, recurring schedule or guaranteed
  outcome is introduced. Product decisions and evidence remain product-owned.
- Author case studies, traffic-share claims and uncontrolled performance changes
  are not adopted as predictions or causal evidence.

The dated official links supporting platform corrections are maintained with the
relevant references. Revisit them when the recommendation depends on current behavior.
