# Naver search

Use this reference when the product actually targets Naver audiences. Verify
current requirements through Naver Search Advisor rather than extending a Google
rule or an author's case study to Naver.

- Review the intended site's ownership verification, accessible pages, Yeti
  policy and submitted sitemap using the approved account and product context.
  If account access is unavailable, report it and prepare the exact next step.
- Use the reports actually available in that account, preserving report names,
  dimensions, time ranges and retrieval times. Do not assume Google-style query,
  click or position exports exist in every Naver tool.
- Check useful content, sources and mobile presentation on relevant pages. If
  AI Briefing visibility is a goal, record actual searches and cited URLs as
  samples. Do not treat purported citation factors as established requirements.
- Evaluate an external Naver blog from audience and operating needs. Keep
  product facts and source material in their owning location. Verify the editor
  and published mobile result when publication is authorized.

Do not infer a fixed Naver share of a product's traffic, mandatory inside/outside
strategy, or a weekly automation from this reference. Avoid artificial reciprocal
promotion, repetitive comments and mass distribution for search manipulation.
